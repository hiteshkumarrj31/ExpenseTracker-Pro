Privacy Policy

Last updated:

At ExpenseTracker Pro, we take your privacy very seriously. Our philosophy is simple: your financial data belongs to you, and no one else.

1. Data Storage

All data entered into ExpenseTracker Pro (including income, expenses, categories, settings, and PIN) is stored locally on your device using your browser's Local Storage. We do not use external databases, servers, or cloud storage to host your financial data.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or financial data. Everything happens completely offline once the application is loaded.

3. Security

Since your data lives on your device, its security depends on your device's security. We provide an optional App PIN Lock feature to prevent unauthorized access if someone else uses your device. However, we cannot recover your PIN or data if you lose them, as we have no access to them.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the app functions entirely offline, any policy updates will be reflected in future software updates.
